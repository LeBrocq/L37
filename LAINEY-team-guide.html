Latitude 37 How we work with AI

Using LAINEY

A short guide for everyone. What LAINEY is, where you work, what you can and cannot put into it, and what it is genuinely good at today.

Draft for leadership review August 2026 Read with the AI Policy and the AI Playbook

01

What LAINEY is

LAINEY is Latitude 37's knowledge, written down in one place, so that AI reads what we know before it writes anything for us.

It holds our brand story and voice, how we talk about our work, who our clients are, how the business runs, and a defined method for each kind of work we produce. When you ask ChatGPT for a client email or an article, it reads the relevant part of LAINEY first. That is the difference between output that sounds like anyone and output that sounds like us.

LAINEY is not a separate app and there is nothing new to log into. You will never open the place it is stored. You reach it through ChatGPT.

02

The one rule

Only information that could be shared with everyone in the company goes into LAINEY.

Everything else stays where it already lives. That single rule is why LAINEY can be open to the whole team, and why it is safe to connect to AI at all. If you are ever unsure about something, that is the question to ask: could this be sent to everyone at Latitude 37?

Client-specific material never enters LAINEY. No names, no personal details, no contract values. Project codes only. Client records stay in HubSpot, and project documents stay in the project sites where permissions are already enforced.

What AI is allowed to do

Read freely. Draft anything. Send, publish or spend, never.

ChatGPT can look across the systems you already have access to and use that context to write. It can never do anything that leaves the building or costs money without a person doing it. That is deliberate, and it is what lets you use it confidently rather than carefully.

05

Before you prompt

  • Am I in an approved tool, on my Latitude 37 account, not a personal one?
  • Am I typing or pasting anything that identifies a client or a project?
  • Am I sharing only what the task actually needs?
  • Will I check the output before it goes anywhere?
  • Am I still the one making the decision?

Checking the output means checking the things that can be wrong and matter: facts, numbers, dates, drawing references, scope, and anything that reads as a commitment to a client. AI is confident when it is wrong. That is the whole reason the check exists.

06

Stop

Stop and ask if

  • You are about to type or paste client details, pricing, margins, contract terms, drawings or personal information into a chat.
  • The task is a decision about pricing, contracts, engineering, safety, legal or employment. AI prepares. People decide.
  • You are about to use a tool that is not on the approved list.
  • Something has gone out that should not have, or you think information may have been exposed.
  • You are not sure. Being unsure is a good enough reason to ask, and asking is never the wrong call.

AI may never approve a design, a price, a contract, an engineering or safety judgement, or a commitment to a client. It can prepare all of them for a person to decide.

07

What LAINEY is good at today

LAINEY is partly built. Being straight about that is more useful than overselling it, because knowing where it is strong is how you get good work out of it in the first week.

Reliable now

Brand, voice, positioning, messaging and how we talk about our clients. Ask for a client email, an article, a case for something, a piece of website or campaign copy, and it will sound like us.

Not there yet

How the business actually runs. Build process, team, systems, clients, sales process and finance are mostly still empty templates. Ask about those and you will get generic output.

Reliable now

The method. There is a defined process for each of 46 kinds of work, from a milestone email to a subcontractor brief to a monthly report. Ask for the work by name and it follows our method.

Not there yet

Project records. No completed project has been written up, so anything that depends on real project detail, case studies, testimonials, awards, will be thin until they are.

If output comes back generic, it is usually because the part of LAINEY it needed has not been written yet. That is worth reporting, not working around.

08

Getting better output

Ask for the work by name

There is a defined method behind most of what we produce. Asking for a milestone email, an enquiry response, a defects communication or a suburb guide gets you our process. Asking vaguely gets you a generic version of the same thing.

Give it the context it cannot see

Stage, audience, what has already been agreed, what is still open, what you do not want it to say. Most poor output is an underfed prompt rather than a bad tool.

Start small

Use the smallest relevant extract rather than uploading whole document sets. It is faster, cheaper and more accurate.

Watch the effort setting

Higher reasoning settings take longer and use our shared allowance faster. Match the setting to the task. Most everyday drafting does not need the heaviest setting.

09

When something is wrong, or missing

  • Output is wrong or generic. Report it. It usually means something in LAINEY is missing or out of date, and it will stay wrong for everyone until someone says so.
  • Something should become company knowledge. Send it to the administrator. Only they can publish into LAINEY, which is what keeps it trustworthy.
  • You have found a use that works well. Share it. That is how the Playbook improves.
  • Something has gone wrong with information. Report it immediately. Early is always better.

Nothing changes in LAINEY without approval, and every change is recorded and reversible. That is what stops it drifting.
