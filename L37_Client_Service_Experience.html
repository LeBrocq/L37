The preferred lane

The process is the preferred lane. L37 holds it by default and leaves it only where the client's outcome genuinely needs it. Deciding when to deviate is judgement, not rigidity. That is why the key agreements work best as the 'lane' (preferred), not a 'fence' (fixed): the preferred way of doing things that the team is trusted to depart from when the client's outcome warrants it, rather than as unbreakable rules that turn every non-standard request into a refusal.
